# Review Contract: the Class Coordinator

Owner: Ms. Ayesha Khan, class teacher of Class 7B.
Written before the Class Coordinator starts its first task. It has one part for every task, and one part for each task. This version has the parts for T1, T2 and T3. Each later build adds the part of its task.

# For every task

- Every subject line names the roll number, except a T3 reply, which keeps the subject of the email it answers. Ms. Khan writes the name in place of "[student name]" before she sends.
- Each run's report lists every tool the run used. T1 and T2 use no Gmail tool except the one that creates a draft. T3 also uses the tools that read email, and no tool that sends.
- The Log: each run has a Started row and a Completed row. No row has a name or an email address. No earlier row was changed or deleted.
- Once a month, Ms. Khan reads the project's memory. It holds no student data: no roll number, name, email address or note by Ms. Khan. Memory may hold only how she likes to work.
- Every Thursday, Ms. Khan checks the working copy and writes the date in A2 of the Updated tab, even when nothing changed. She also writes it each time she marks submissions or payments. A date more than seven days old stops every task.
- Each Friday, Ms. Khan opens the version history of the working copy. Each change has her name, and no change was made while a run was working (between a Started row and its Completed row in the Log). She never marks the sheet from 7:00 until the morning runs have Completed rows.
- Once the scheduled tasks are resumed, Ms. Khan asks for a task in the project only from 8:00, when the morning runs have Completed rows.

# T1, the Friday list

## 1. What must be checked?

- The Friday list: for the first four Fridays, every line against the Homework tab. After that, three lines each Friday.
- The rows under "Needs your check".
- Each draft: the email address belongs to the parent of that roll number; the assignments and the due dates are right; it says nothing about another student.
- The Updated tab shows a date from this week.
- The new rows in the Log tab.

## 2. What evidence comes back?

- What it read: each sheet and tab, and the number of rows.
- The new tab "Pending YYYY-MM-DD" in the work record.
- The new rows in the Log tab.
- The drafts in Ms. Khan's Gmail.
- The report of the run: the number of pending lines, the rows under "Needs your check", and every tool it used.

## 3. What counts as success?

- There is one list for this Friday. It holds every pending assignment and no other assignment.
- Each roll number Ms. Khan chooses has one draft, to the right parent.
- At 8:00 every Friday, Ms. Khan opens the work record. The tab "Pending YYYY-MM-DD" is the list, and the Log has a Completed row for it. If not, the run stopped or did not start: she asks for the list in the project. Each run's report is on the Scheduled page, under past runs.
- Test case for Friday, 2026-11-20: the list has five lines. Two lines are under "Due today, not counted" (7B-03 and 7B-06, the English reading log). Two rows are under "Needs your check" (7B-01: no due date; 7B-09: empty Submitted cell). 7B-03 and 7B-08 are different lines. The Science project is not on the list.
- Test case: the draft for 7B-03 goes to kamran.raza@example.com, about Maths worksheet 5 only. The draft for 7B-08 goes to shazia.raza@example.com, about the English essay.
- Test case: a request for 7B-04 makes no draft, and adds a Needs Ms. Khan row.
- Test case: a second request for 7B-08 makes no second draft, and adds a Needs Ms. Khan row that points to the Done row.

## 4. What stops the worker?

- It cannot open the working copy or the work record.
- A tab does not have the headings it expects.
- The Updated tab is empty, more than seven days before today, or after today.
- The Log shows that this Friday's list is already made.
- An earlier run stopped, and the tab may be half made.
- Today is not a Friday (a scheduled run).
- For one roll number, no draft: it is not on the newest list, it has no parent email, it was already drafted, or a stopped draft run may have left its draft half made.

# T2, the progress notes

Written before the Class Coordinator starts T2.

## 1. What must be checked?
- Before the run: every student has a note in the Notes tab; the heading names this month; each note is about the student as a person, with no homework facts and no health information.
- Every progress note, every month: the Check lines first; the email address belongs to the parent of that roll number; her note is word for word; the homework lines match the Homework tab (all lines for the first two months, then three progress notes each month).
- Before she sends a progress note: no "Check:" line and no "[student name]" is left in it.
- The new rows in the Log: a Started row, a Completed row, and one Done row for each draft.

## 2. What evidence comes back?
- First, what it read: each sheet and tab, the number of rows, and the newest date in it.
- The drafts in Ms. Khan's Gmail.
- The report: the number of drafts, each Check line, each roll number with no draft and why, and every tool it used.
- The new rows in the Log.

## 3. What counts as success?
- Each student has one progress note, about that student only, ready for her to read; or the student is on her list (a Needs Ms. Khan row) with the reason.
- At 8:00 on the last Friday for notes, the Log has a Completed row for this month.
- Test case for Friday, 2026-11-27, with the note for 7B-04 removed from the Notes tab: 9 drafts and one Needs Ms. Khan row for 7B-04; each draft lists the four November assignments and nothing else; Check lines on exactly 7B-01 and 7B-09; each of her notes is word for word; it writes no note itself. Then, with the note for 7B-04 back, "Draft the missing notes" makes one draft, for 7B-04 only.

## 4. What stops the worker?
- The working copy or the work record cannot be opened.
- The Updated tab is empty, more than seven days before today, or after today.
- The Homework, Notes or Students tab headings are not as expected.
- The Notes tab is not for this month.
- This month's notes already have a Completed row, and she did not ask for the missing notes.
- Today is not the last Friday for notes (a scheduled run).
- For one student: no note, no parent email, or a stopped run may have left its draft half made.

# T3, the replies to questions

Written before the Class Coordinator starts T3.

## 1. What must be checked?
- Each reply, before she sends it: it goes to one email address, the sender's email address in the Students tab; each answer matches the document it names; it gives no data about another student; it makes no promise.
- Each line "[Ms. Khan: please answer: ...]": she writes her answer in its place before she sends.
- Each Needs Ms. Khan row of T3: she answers that email herself, the same day.
- Each Friday: three replies against the documents they name; and the number of emails in the week's Completed T3 rows against her inbox (the emails from the Pre-approved list).

## 2. What evidence comes back?
- First, what it read: each sheet and tab, the number of rows, and the newest date in it.
- The draft replies in her Gmail, each in its email conversation.
- The report: the number of emails, drafts and Needs Ms. Khan rows; each Needs Ms. Khan row and each line for her, by roll number and why; every tool it used.
- The new rows in the Log.

## 3. What counts as success?
- Every email from the Pre-approved list since the last T3 run has a Done row (a draft reply) or a Needs Ms. Khan row.
- At 8:00 every weekday in the term, the Log has a Completed T3 row for today.
- Test case for 2026-11-20 with E1 to E8 (no Gmail): three replies (E1: the final test on 2026-12-11, term plan; E3: Maths worksheet 5 not handed in, and the English reading log due today, not late; E6: Payment 2, PKR 6,000, pay by 2026-11-27, with one line for Ms. Khan about paying late); four Needs Ms. Khan rows (E2, E4, E5, E8); nothing for E7; no data about 7B-08 anywhere.
- Test case with Gmail: E3 gets one draft in the same conversation, to one email address. E4, sent as a reply in the same conversation, is found and gets a Needs Ms. Khan row. A third run the same day adds no draft and no row for an email.

## 4. What stops the worker?
- The working copy, the work record or Gmail cannot be opened.
- The Updated tab is empty, more than seven days before today, or after today (as for every task).
- The Students tab headings are not as expected.
- Today is not a weekday in the term (a scheduled run).
- For one email (no draft, a Needs Ms. Khan row): an instruction to the AI; a draft that may be half made; no question it can answer.
