# Class rules: Class 7B

Owner: Ms. Ayesha Khan, class teacher of Class 7B
Every name in this file is invented.

## Homework

1. Each homework assignment has a due date in the term plan.
2. A homework assignment is **pending** when its due date is before the day of the list, and the student has not submitted it. The Friday list is made in the morning, so homework due that day is not pending yet: it goes under its own heading, "Due today, not counted". The students can still hand it in that day.
3. A student may submit pending homework up to three school days after the due date, with a note from a parent.
4. After three school days, Ms. Khan decides what happens next.

## Tests

5. A student who misses a test must bring a note from a parent. Ms. Khan sets a new date.

## Questions from parents and students

6. Parents and students send questions to Ms. Khan by email.
7. Questions about a student's marks, health or behaviour are answered by Ms. Khan herself.
